01 / THE MAISON

A DIFFERENT MEASURE OF VALUE

Luxury is not simply
what an object is.
It is what it means.

Folle Orfèvre is a contemporary independent maison exploring sculptural jewellery and collectible objects. We draw inspiration from the discipline of European goldsmithing and the freedom of artistic invention.

Our ambition is to create pieces with a point of view: considered in form, deliberate in detail and made to hold meaning beyond a moment.

03 / THE CHRONICLE

A WORLD OF OUR OWN

The unfinished
is not the incomplete.

The Chronicle of the Impossible is an original fictional universe created for the artistic identity of Folle Orfèvre. It follows a mysterious goldsmith, six symbolic creations and the seventh object that was never made.

Inspired by the broader history and aesthetics of European goldsmithing, the Chronicle is a work of imagination—not a claim of historical succession, provenance or affiliation.
