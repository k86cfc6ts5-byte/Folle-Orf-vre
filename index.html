01 / THE MAISON

A DIFFERENT MEASURE OF VALUE

Luxury is not simply
what an object is.
It is what it means.

Folle Orfèvre is a contemporary independent maison exploring sculptural jewellery and collectible objects. We draw inspiration from the discipline of European goldsmithing and the freedom of artistic invention.

Our ambition is to create pieces with a point of view: considered in form, deliberate in detail and made to hold meaning beyond a moment.

03 / THE CHRONICLE

THE LEGEND OF THE MAISON

The unfinished
is not the incomplete.

The Chronicle of the Impossible is the founding legend of Folle Orfèvre: a mysterious goldsmith, six symbolic creations and the seventh object that was never made.

It is set among the goldsmiths of revolutionary Paris, whose workshops were scattered and whose archives were lost as an old world fell. The maison carries the legend forward—not as a claim of lineage, but as a measure to make objects against.
