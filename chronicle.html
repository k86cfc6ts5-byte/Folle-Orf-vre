A FICTIONAL CHRONICLE

The seventh
absence.

Six creations. One impossible absence.

In the mythology of Folle Orfèvre, a mysterious goldsmith begins a sequence of six creations. Each is an expression of a human quality: courage, will, time, memory, imagination and integrity.

The six silences

The objects become the centre of a hidden circle of makers, patrons and keepers. Their purpose is questioned; their meaning is disputed. In time, the creations disappear from one another, scattered into the world.

Six can be crafted. One must be earned.

The seventh

The seventh creation is never completed. It is not a physical object or a treasure to be recovered. It is the life one leads, and the legacy one leaves behind.

This Chronicle is original fiction created for the Folle Orfèvre brand universe. The goldsmith, society, objects and events are fictional and are not historical facts, provenance, or a claim of French origin or succession.
