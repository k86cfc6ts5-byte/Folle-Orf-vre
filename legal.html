Who we are

Folle Orfèvre creations are marketed and distributed by Bhavya Anand Shakti Peeth (BASP) Religious and Welfare Trust, Ranchi, Jharkhand, India.

Enquiries

An enquiry is the beginning of a private conversation with the maison. It is not an order, reservation or contract, and no payment is requested or collected through this website. Each creation is made to order; the final specification, materials, price, applicable taxes and timing are confirmed in writing before anything is agreed.

Your information

When you submit an enquiry, we collect your name, email address, country, optional telephone number, the creation of interest and any note you choose to include. We use this only to reply to you and to discuss your enquiry. We do not sell or share it for marketing.

Please do not send payment card details, identification numbers or other sensitive information through the form. You may ask us at any time to correct or delete the information you have shared.

The Register

Owners of first-edition pieces may choose to be named in the Register, unveiled at midnight on 31 December 2026 (IST). Being named is entirely optional, requires separate explicit consent, and is never a condition of an enquiry or purchase. Only confirmed owners who have consented will appear.

The Chronicle

The Chronicle of the Impossible is the maison’s founding legend, told in the spirit of the goldsmiths of eighteenth-century Paris. Folle Orfèvre is a contemporary independent maison and does not claim descent from, or affiliation with, any historical workshop or jewellery house.

Materials

Unless a quotation states otherwise, creations are made in 925 sterling silver with 18k gold plating and set with lab-created gemstones and cubic zirconia. Gold plating is a surface finish and will soften with wear; the maison offers re-plating on request.

Editions & authenticity

Each design is limited to 100 numbered pieces. Every piece is issued with a signed, numbered certificate of authenticity recording its design, edition number and materials. Once an edition is complete, the design is not remade.

Designs & intellectual property

The designs, names, imagery and text of Folle Orfèvre are the intellectual property of the maison. Unauthorised reproduction or imitation will be pursued under the laws of the country in which it is made, offered or sold.

Prices

Prices are set in US dollars. Amounts shown in Indian rupees, euros and UAE dirhams are indicative conversions at reference rates and may differ from the final quoted price. All prices exclude applicable taxes.
