Fiction disclosure

The Chronicle of the Impossible, including its goldsmith, hidden circle, six creations and historical narrative, is fictional brand storytelling. Folle Orfèvre does not claim historical French origin, succession from an 18th-century workshop, or ownership of historical artefacts.

Enquiries are not orders

Submitting an enquiry expresses interest only. It is not a purchase, reservation, confirmed allocation or offer acceptance. No checkout or payment is processed on this website. Final product specifications, pricing, taxes, delivery and sale terms must be confirmed directly before a purchase is agreed.

Privacy and enquiry data

Information submitted through the enquiry form is intended to be used to review and respond to the enquiry. Access should be restricted to authorised maison personnel. Do not submit payment credentials or sensitive personal information. A separate optional consent is requested before a confirmed buyer's name may be published in the First Edition announcement. Refusing publication does not affect consideration of an enquiry.

Before opening enquiries publicly, add verified legal entity details, a privacy contact, data retention period, processor information and jurisdiction-specific disclosures. Contact details and legal particulars are intentionally not fabricated here.

Product concepts, materials, specifications and prices are proposed and indicative until verified with the manufacturer and formally confirmed. No material, hallmark, origin, edition quantity or delivery claim should be relied on until confirmed in writing.
