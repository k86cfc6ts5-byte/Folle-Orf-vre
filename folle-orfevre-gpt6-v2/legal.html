The Chronicle & Its Origins

The Chronicles of the Impossible, including Le Fol Orfèvre, Les Sept Folies, Les Insensés, the six creations, the seventh creation, all associated characters, organisations, events, letters and historical narratives, constitute an original fictional universe created for the artistic identity and storytelling of Folle Orfèvre.

The narrative draws creative inspiration from the history, aesthetics, craftsmanship and cultural traditions of European goldsmithing and decorative arts. Its characters, events, artefacts and claimed historical connections are fictional unless expressly identified as independently verified historical facts.

Folle Orfèvre is a contemporary independent maison. It does not represent itself as the successor, owner, continuation or historical affiliate of the fictional workshop or any historical jewellery house.

The Chronicle is a work of creative fiction and must not be interpreted as evidence of historical provenance, ownership, manufacturing origin, authenticity, certification or affiliation.

All representations concerning actual products, materials, artisans, manufacturing locations, edition quantities, provenance, certificates and ownership rights are separate from the fictional universe and are subject to their own factual documentation.

Enquiry information & privacy

When you submit an enquiry, the information you provide—including your name, email address, country, optional telephone number, selected creation and message—is collected to review and respond to your enquiry and, where relevant, to discuss a potential limited-edition allocation.

Submitting an enquiry does not create an order, reservation, purchase contract or guaranteed allocation. No payment details are requested or collected through the enquiry form.

The option to publish your name in the 31 December 2026 announcement is separate and optional. It is not a condition of submitting an enquiry or being considered. A name will not be published under this programme unless the individual has affirmatively selected the publication consent option and later becomes a confirmed buyer.

Enquiry records are intended to be accessible only to authorised personnel who administer the maison's enquiries. Before public launch, Folle Orfèvre must publish its verified legal entity details, privacy contact, applicable retention period, data processor information and any additional notices required by the laws applicable to its visitors.

Do not submit payment card details, government identification numbers or other sensitive personal information through this form.

The legend is fictional. The objects, craftsmanship and commitments of the maison must be real.
