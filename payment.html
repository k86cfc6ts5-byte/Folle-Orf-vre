PRIVATE CLIENT · PAYMENT & ORDERING

Payment, by arrangement.

Folle Orfèvre does not sell through an online checkout. Every creation is made to order, and payment is only ever requested against a written quotation and a signed agreement.

IMPORTANT

Only ever
by invoice.

The maison will never request payment through social media messages, unsolicited calls, or bank or wallet details that do not appear on your official invoice.

If you receive a payment request you are unsure of, do not pay. Write to the maison through the enquiry form quoting your reference, and we will confirm it personally.

  1. I

    Enquiry

    Tell us which creation interests you. There is no obligation and nothing is charged.

  2. II

    Consultation

    The maison contacts you privately to discuss the piece, sizing, materials and timing, by video call or in person.

  3. III

    Written quotation

    You receive a quotation with the final specification, price, applicable taxes and the delivery window, in your preferred currency.

  4. IV

    Agreement & deposit

    Once you approve the design, you sign a reservation agreement and pay a deposit against a secure invoice. The agreement sets out when the deposit is refundable.

  5. V

    Making & balance

    Your piece is made. The balance is invoiced only when it is finished and you have approved it.

  6. VI

    Delivery

    Insured delivery or private handover, with an invoice and documentation for your piece.

ACCEPTED METHODS

Payment is made through a secure invoice link from a regulated payment provider, which accepts major cards, bank transfer and UPI. The link is sent to you personally with your invoice and is valid only for that invoice.
