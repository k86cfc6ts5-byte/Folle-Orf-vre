Our creations are offered in limited editions. Tell us which piece has caught your attention. Every enquiry is read personally, and the maison replies privately to discuss specification, sizing and timing.

HOW IT WORKS

Considered.
Never automatic.

An enquiry is simply the beginning of a conversation. It is not an order, and nothing is charged on this website.

Each creation is made to order. Should you wish to proceed, the maison will share the final specification, materials, price, taxes and timing in writing before anything is agreed.

Prices are shown in INR and are indicative, exclusive of applicable taxes.

Please do not include payment details or sensitive personal information. An enquiry is not an order.
